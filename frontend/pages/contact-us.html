How long does Nellai Specialz Halwa stay fresh? ▼

Our Original Wheat Halwa stays fresh for up to 25–30 days at room temperature when stored in an airtight container. Karupatti Halwa has a shelf life of up to 45 days thanks to natural palm jaggery preservation.

Do you deliver across India & International destinations? ▼

Yes! We ship across all pincodes in India with express courier service (delivery within 2-4 business days). International bulk orders can be coordinated via our WhatsApp support team.

Are white sugar or artificial colors used in Karupatti Halwa? ▼

No artificial colors, preservatives, or refined white sugar are added to our Karupatti Halwa. It is prepared 100% naturally using authentic palm jaggery, pure cow ghee, and hand-extracted wheat milk.
