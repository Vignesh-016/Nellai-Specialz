Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

1. Freshness & Express Dispatch

Every order of Tirunelveli Halwa is freshly prepared in traditional brass vessels and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation to ensure optimum taste and aroma.

2. Delivery Timelines Across India

  • Tamil Nadu & Puducherry: 1 - 2 Business Days
  • South India (Kerala, Karnataka, AP, Telangana): 2 - 3 Business Days
  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata): 3 - 4 Business Days
  • Rest of India: 4 - 5 Business Days

3. Food-Grade Vacuum Packaging

To preserve softness and freshness during transit, our Halwa is sealed in food-grade, multi-layer leak-proof containers designed for long-distance travel without artificial preservatives.

4. Order Tracking

Once dispatched, you will receive an SMS and WhatsApp update containing your courier tracking number and real-time live location details.
