Return & Refunds Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

1. Freshness & Quality Assurance

Because our sweets are perishable food items prepared fresh without preservatives, we do not accept general returns once a package has been opened.

2. Replacement for Damaged Parcels

If your package arrives physically damaged or tampered with during transit, please take a photo/video of the box upon delivery and notify us via WhatsApp at +91 93841 12345 within 24 hours. We will immediately arrange a free replacement parcel.

3. Refund Processing

Approved refund claims will be credited back to your original source of payment within 3 to 5 business days.
