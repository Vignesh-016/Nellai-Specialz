The Nellai Specialz story

How Nellai Specialz began

Our story begins with the flavours that make Tirunelveli feel like home: generous, familiar and made with patience.

Nellai Specialz brings together authentic sweets and traditional snacks with a simple purpose — to preserve the taste people know and love, and share it with more homes.

From carefully chosen ingredients to thoughtful packing, every detail is guided by freshness, quality and care.

The halwa story

What makes Tirunelveli halwa special?

Its appeal is in the balance: a rich texture, a distinctive Tirunelveli taste and the patience behind traditional preparation. It is a sweet that carries a sense of place.

We honour that character with quality ingredients, careful preparation and packing that helps the experience reach you fresh.
