Tirunelveli Halwa is world-renowned for its distinct dark amber sheen, rich ghee aroma, and soft texture. At Nellai Specialz, we strictly adhere to the centuries-old traditional recipe without shortcuts or modern artificial thickeners.

Key Ingredients

  • Whole Sambha Wheat (Milk Extracted)
  • Pure Country Cow Ghee
  • Thamirabarani River Water
  • Pure Cane Sugar & Cardamom
  • Roasted Cashew Nuts

Storage & Shelf Life

  • Shelf Life: 25 - 30 days at room temperature.
  • Do not refrigerate (ghee will solidify).
  • Warm slightly before serving for maximum softness & aroma.
