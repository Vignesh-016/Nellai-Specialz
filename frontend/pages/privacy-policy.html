Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

1. Data Collection & Privacy Commitment

At Nellai Specialz, we respect your privacy. We collect customer names, shipping addresses, email IDs, and phone numbers strictly for order fulfillment, delivery tracking, and essential service communications.

2. Secure Payment Processing

We do not store credit/debit card details or UPI credentials on our servers. All transactions are securely processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

3. Non-Disclosure to Third Parties

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to third-party marketing companies. Data is shared exclusively with our verified logistics partners solely for parcel delivery.

If you have any questions regarding your personal data, please contact our support team at hello@nellaispecialz.com.
